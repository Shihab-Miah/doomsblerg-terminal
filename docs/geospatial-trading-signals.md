## Signal Map: What Physical Events Move Which Markets

### 🚢 Ships & Maritime (AISStream.io — Free)

| What You See | What It Signals | Which Market Moves |
|---|---|---|
| Oil tankers slowing down or waiting outside Strait of Hormuz | Potential blockage / tension → supply disruption | Crude oil futures (WTI, Brent) go UP |
| VLCCs (huge crude tankers) idling near Singapore | Floating storage buildup → oversupply | Oil price goes DOWN |
| Container ships queuing at Port of LA/LB | Supply chain backup → inventory delays | Retail stocks (Walmart, Target, Amazon) at risk |
| LNG tankers diverting from Europe to Asia | Energy arbitrage shift | European natural gas prices rise |
| Grain bulk carriers loaded up in Brazil | Big export surge coming | Soybean/corn futures may drop (more supply) |
| Ship transponder goes dark near sanctioned country | Potential sanctions evasion (Russia/Iran oil) | Do NOT use this for trading — legal/ethical concern |
